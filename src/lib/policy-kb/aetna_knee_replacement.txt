AETNA CLINICAL POLICY BULLETIN
Total Knee Replacement / Arthroplasty
Policy Number: 0000 (Sample)
CPT: 27447

MEDICAL NECESSITY

Aetna considers total knee arthroplasty (TKA) medically necessary for members with osteoarthritis or other advanced joint disease of the knee when ALL of the following criteria are met:

1. Radiographic evidence of moderate to severe osteoarthritis (Kellgren-Lawrence grade 3 or 4) or equivalent findings on MRI/CT showing joint space narrowing, osteophytes, subchondral sclerosis or cysts, or bone-on-bone articulation.

2. Documented functional disability due to knee pain for at least 3 months that interferes with activities of daily living (e.g., walking, climbing stairs, rising from chair).

3. Failure of at least 3 months of conservative non-operative management including:
   - Physical therapy (minimum 6 sessions or documented completion of a supervised program)
   - Pharmacologic therapy (NSAIDs, acetaminophen, or other analgesics) unless contraindicated
   - At least one course of intra-articular corticosteroid injection (unless contraindicated or refused)
   - Activity modification and/or weight loss counseling for overweight members

4. No contraindications to surgery (active infection, severe medical comorbidities that make surgery high risk without optimization).

SPECIAL NOTES
- Bilateral simultaneous TKA may be considered when both knees meet criteria and the member is optimized for surgery.
- Revision TKA requires documentation of implant failure, loosening, infection, or other mechanical complication.
- Prior authorization is required for elective TKA performed in outpatient or inpatient settings.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION
- Imaging reports (X-ray preferred; MRI if recent)
- Physical therapy notes with dates and outcomes
- Medication trial history
- Office notes documenting pain and functional limitations
