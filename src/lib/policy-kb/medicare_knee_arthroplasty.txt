MEDICARE PRIOR AUTHORIZATION GUIDELINES
Total Knee Arthroplasty (TKA) / Total Knee Replacement
CPT Codes: 27447, 27486, 27487
LCD Reference: L36575 / L40232 (Total Joint Arthroplasty)

COVERAGE INDICATIONS AND MEDICAL NECESSITY

Primary total knee arthroplasty is considered reasonable and medically necessary if ALL of the following criteria have been met:

1. Advanced joint disease of the knee evidenced by conventional radiography, MRI, or CT demonstrating one or more of the following:
   - Subchondral cysts
   - Subchondral sclerosis
   - Periarticular osteophytes
   - Joint subluxation
   - Joint space narrowing
   - Avascular necrosis
   - Bone-on-bone articulations

2. Pain with functional disability at the knee that interferes with activities of daily living (ADLs), documented as:
   - Pain increased with initiation of activities
   - Pain that increases with weight bearing
   - Pain precluding sleep
   - Inability to perform household chores or prolonged standing

3. History of a reasonable attempt (typically 3 months or more) at conservative therapy appropriate for the patient in the current episode of care, including documented trial of:
   - NSAIDs or contraindication to such therapy
   - Supervised physical therapy (at least 3 sessions documented)
   - Activity modification
   - Weight management counseling when BMI > 30
   - Intra-articular corticosteroid injections (when appropriate)

4. The procedure does not exceed the medical need and is at least as beneficial as existing alternatives.

5. Documentation must clearly support the diagnostic criteria and clinical findings. Progress notes with only conclusive statements are insufficient.

REVISION / REPLACEMENT TKA

Revision or replacement TKA is medically necessary when documentation shows cause of failure of the primary procedure such as:
- Infection
- Aseptic loosening
- Periprosthetic fracture
- Instability
- Moderate to severe pain and loss of function
- Polyethylene wear
- Restriction of motion / arthrofibrosis
- Extensor mechanism insufficiency
- Implant failure
- Allergy to implant components

DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
- Radiographic or advanced imaging reports
- Detailed history of conservative treatments tried and failed, with dates and duration
- Physical exam findings including range of motion, strength, gait
- Functional assessment scores if available
- Operative notes for revision cases

EXCLUSIONS
- Unicompartmental (partial) knee replacement is not covered under the same criteria as total knee arthroplasty in some LCDs.
- Procedures performed solely for cosmetic reasons or without medical necessity documentation.
